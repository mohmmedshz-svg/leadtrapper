## What is LeadTrapper?

LeadTrapper is a B2B lead generation platform built for businesses in Dubai, Riyadh, Cairo, London, and beyond. It scrapes verified business leads from Google Maps, Instagram, and Facebook — complete with phone numbers and emails — then sends WhatsApp bulk messages to thousands of contacts at once.

## Key Features

- **Google Maps Scraper** — Extract business names, phone numbers, emails, and addresses from any Google Maps search
- **Instagram Scraper** — Scrape leads from hashtags, locations, and competitor followers
- **Facebook Scraper** — Extract leads from Facebook pages and groups
- **WhatsApp Bulk Sender** — Send personalized WhatsApp messages to thousands of leads
- **Lead Deduplication** — Automatically removes duplicate contacts
- **Export to CSV** — Download all leads for use in any CRM
